# Bot roster (the ops team)

The full name is the namesake. Log lines use the short name (the slug).

Read only your own block: `bots/<slug>/block.md`. One bot, one job.
A new bot gets its heading here and its block file in the same change. `scripts/check.py` rejects a log line from a bot that has no block.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chief | Stafford Beer | Runs the fleet: rules, routing, audits, stuck cards | Stafford Beer: the purpose of a system is what it does, so audit bots by what they do. Also inspired by the Alfred marketplace bot | `bots/chief/block.md` |
