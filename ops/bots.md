# Bot roster (the ops team)

Read only your own block: `bots/<slug>/block.md`. One bot, one job.
A new bot gets its heading here and its block file in the same change. `scripts/check.py` rejects a log line from a bot that has no block.

| Bot | Job in one line | Namesake idea | Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief | Runs the fleet: rules, routing, audits, stuck cards | Alfred (marketplace bot; smallest useful structure) | `bots/chief/block.md` |
| Deming | Reviewer. Second check before anything leaves the team; turns repeated answers into rules | W. Edwards Deming: quality is built into the process | `bots/deming/block.md` |
| Goldratt | Orders all work by the bottleneck | Eliyahu Goldratt, Theory of Constraints | `bots/goldratt/block.md` |
| Meadows | Models one piece of work as a system and proposes one structural fix | Donella Meadows, leverage points | `bots/meadows/block.md` |
| Munger | Money math and spend, weekly | Charlie Munger: invert, check the incentives | `bots/munger/block.md` |
| Engelbart | Watches the platform's changelogs and best practices | Douglas Engelbart: augment the team's capability | `bots/engelbart/block.md` |
| Simon | Bot builder. Drafts new bots from the template | Herbert Simon: satisficing, one small job per bot | `bots/simon/block.md` |

Rename them. The names are labels, not endorsements (see README, "Names").
